# Quantum Computing Basics

Quantum computing harnesses the phenomena of quantum mechanics, such as superposition and entanglement.

## Qubits and Superposition

Unlike classical bits which are 0 or 1, a qubit exists in a linear combination state: |psi> = alpha |0> + beta |1|.

## Quantum Entanglement

Entangled qubits exhibit correlated states regardless of physical separation distance.
